Tea varieties

Black tea is a fully oxidized tea made from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant. It generally has a strong flavor, dark color, and noticeable aroma. Common examples include Assam, Darjeeling, Ceylon, Keemun, and English Breakfast. Black tea can be consumed plain or with milk, sugar, lemon, spices, or other flavorings. It is commonly served hot but can also be used to prepare iced tea.

Assam tea is a black tea associated with the Assam region of India. It is known for its strong, malty, brisk, and full-bodied character. Assam tea is commonly used in breakfast tea blends and Indian milk tea. It pairs well with milk and sugar and is an important ingredient in many styles of masala chai.

Darjeeling tea is a tea produced in the Darjeeling region of West Bengal, India. It is usually a black tea, although different processing styles can produce other types. Darjeeling tea is often described as aromatic, floral, fruity, and delicate. First-flush Darjeeling is harvested early in the season and is generally lighter and more floral, while later flushes can have a fuller and more rounded character.

Nilgiri tea is produced in the Nilgiri Hills of southern India. It is generally a fragrant black tea with a clean, bright, and sometimes floral character. Nilgiri tea is suitable for both hot tea and iced tea. It is also used in tea blends because its flavor can remain noticeable when combined with other teas.

Ceylon tea is tea produced in Sri Lanka, historically known as Ceylon. It is predominantly black tea and can vary significantly depending on the growing region and elevation. Ceylon tea can have citrus, floral, brisk, or full-bodied characteristics. It is commonly served with or without milk and is also widely used for iced tea.

English Breakfast tea is a black tea blend designed to provide a strong, robust cup suitable for breakfast. Blends vary, but they often contain teas from regions such as Assam, Sri Lanka, Kenya, and other tea-producing countries. English Breakfast is commonly served with milk and sugar.

Irish Breakfast tea is a strong black tea blend traditionally associated with Irish tea-drinking culture. It often has a robust and malty profile and can stand up well to milk. It is commonly served hot, with milk and sometimes sugar.

Earl Grey is a flavored black tea traditionally characterized by bergamot citrus flavor. The base tea is commonly black tea, although green, white, oolong, and herbal versions also exist. Earl Grey has a distinctive citrusy, floral aroma and is often consumed plain or with milk, lemon, or sugar.

Lady Grey is a lighter and more citrus-oriented variation of Earl Grey. It typically combines black tea with bergamot and additional citrus notes such as orange or lemon. It generally has a gentler flavor than traditional Earl Grey.

Masala chai is a spiced tea preparation strongly associated with South Asian tea culture. It commonly contains black tea, milk, sugar, and spices such as cardamom, cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and black pepper. Recipes differ by household and region. The tea is usually prepared by simmering or boiling the ingredients together to produce a strong, sweet, aromatic drink.

Chai is a broad term for tea in several languages, but in many English-speaking contexts it refers specifically to Indian-style spiced milk tea. Chai can be prepared using different combinations of black tea, milk, sweetener, and spices. The strength, sweetness, and spice mixture vary widely.

Green tea is tea that is heated or otherwise processed soon after harvesting to limit oxidation. It generally has a lighter color and a fresher flavor than black tea. Depending on its origin and processing, green tea can taste grassy, vegetal, nutty, floral, sweet, or umami-like. It is commonly consumed without milk.

Sencha is a Japanese green tea made from tea leaves that are generally steamed to prevent oxidation and then rolled and dried. It commonly has vegetal, grassy, fresh, and sometimes slightly sweet or umami flavors. Sencha is usually brewed with hot but not boiling water.

Gyokuro is a Japanese shaded green tea. The plants are shaded before harvest, which changes the chemical composition and contributes to its strong umami character. Gyokuro is typically brewed carefully with relatively cooler water and is regarded as a premium Japanese tea style.

Matcha is a finely ground Japanese green tea powder. Unlike ordinary steeped tea, matcha is traditionally whisked into water, meaning the powdered leaf is consumed as part of the drink. Matcha has a vivid green color and can have vegetal, umami, bitter, creamy, and slightly sweet characteristics. It is used in traditional tea preparation as well as modern lattes, desserts, and cold drinks.

Hojicha is a Japanese roasted green tea. The roasting process gives it a brown color and a distinctive toasted, nutty, woody, and caramel-like aroma. Hojicha is often served hot and can also be used in cold beverages and desserts.

Genmaicha is a Japanese green tea blended with roasted rice. Some versions also contain popped rice. The combination produces a characteristic toasted, nutty, savory, and slightly sweet flavor. Genmaicha is commonly served as an everyday tea.

Longjing tea, also called Dragon Well tea, is a famous Chinese green tea associated with Zhejiang Province. The leaves are traditionally pan-fired, producing a flat leaf shape and a fresh, nutty, chestnut-like flavor. High-quality Longjing is often aromatic and smooth.

Gunpowder tea is a Chinese-style green tea in which the leaves are rolled into small pellets. The compact shape helps preserve the tea and creates a distinctive appearance. It generally has a stronger, brisker flavor than some delicate green teas. Gunpowder tea is also used in North African mint tea preparations.

Jasmine tea is tea scented or flavored with jasmine flowers. Green tea is a common base, although white, black, and oolong teas can also be used. The tea has a floral fragrance and can range from delicate to strongly aromatic depending on the production method.

Jasmine green tea combines green tea with jasmine aroma. The tea generally has fresh vegetal notes from the green tea and a floral fragrance from jasmine. It is commonly served hot without milk.

White tea is minimally processed tea made from young leaves and buds. It generally undergoes less processing and oxidation than black tea. White tea can have delicate floral, honey-like, fruity, or mildly grassy flavors. Examples include Silver Needle and White Peony.

Silver Needle is a premium white tea made primarily from young unopened tea buds. It is traditionally associated with China. The resulting infusion is usually pale and delicate, with floral, honey-like, and subtle fruity notes.

White Peony, also known as Bai Mudan, is a Chinese white tea generally made from young buds and leaves. Compared with Silver Needle, it tends to have a fuller flavor and can show floral, fruity, fresh, and slightly woody characteristics.

Oolong tea is partially oxidized tea. Its oxidation level can range from relatively light to quite dark, creating a broad spectrum of flavors. Oolong can be floral, creamy, fruity, roasted, nutty, honey-like, or mineral-like. Major oolong-producing regions include China and Taiwan.

Tieguanyin is a famous Chinese oolong tea associated with Fujian Province. Depending on processing style, it can have floral, creamy, roasted, or vegetal characteristics. Modern lightly roasted versions are often particularly aromatic and floral.

Da Hong Pao is a famous Wuyi oolong from Fujian, China. It is generally roasted and can have complex roasted, mineral, woody, fruity, and nutty characteristics. The name is commonly translated as Big Red Robe.

Dong Ding is a Taiwanese oolong traditionally associated with the Lugu region of Nantou County. It is commonly medium- or heavily roasted and can have toasted, nutty, creamy, fruity, and woody flavors.

Milk oolong is a term used for certain oolong teas that have a naturally creamy or milky sensory character. Some commercial versions may be intentionally flavored, while traditional varieties can develop creamy impressions from the cultivar and processing. Milk oolong is usually smooth and aromatic.

Pu-erh tea is a fermented or post-fermented tea traditionally associated with Yunnan Province in China. It is commonly divided into raw and ripe styles. Raw pu-erh can develop complex floral, fruity, herbal, woody, and earthy characteristics as it ages. Ripe pu-erh generally has darker, smoother, earthy, woody, and sometimes cocoa-like flavors.

Sheng pu-erh is commonly called raw pu-erh. It undergoes a natural aging process after initial production and can change significantly over time. Younger sheng pu-erh may be fresh, bitter, floral, or fruity, while aged examples can become smoother and more complex.

Shou pu-erh is commonly called ripe or cooked pu-erh. It undergoes accelerated microbial fermentation during production. The resulting tea is generally dark, smooth, earthy, woody, and mellow.

Yellow tea is a relatively uncommon tea category. It undergoes a processing step known as yellowing, which gives the leaves and infusion a characteristic yellowish appearance. Yellow tea can have mellow, sweet, floral, nutty, and lightly vegetal characteristics.

Lapsang Souchong is a Chinese black tea traditionally associated with the Wuyi Mountains. It is famous for its smoky aroma and flavor, traditionally produced through smoke drying. Depending on production, it can have pine-smoke, woody, resinous, sweet, and savory notes.

Keemun tea is a Chinese black tea associated with Anhui Province. It is often aromatic and relatively smooth, with notes that may include fruit, malt, cocoa, flowers, or subtle smokiness. It is frequently used in tea blends.

Yunnan black tea is black tea produced in Yunnan Province, China. It can have malty, sweet, floral, honey-like, fruity, and cocoa-like characteristics. Some varieties contain a significant proportion of golden buds.

Rooibos is an herbal infusion made from the South African rooibos plant rather than Camellia sinensis. It naturally contains no caffeine. Rooibos is commonly described as smooth, sweet, nutty, woody, and slightly earthy. It can be served hot or cold and is often used as a base for flavored herbal drinks.

Green rooibos is made from unoxidized rooibos plant material. It generally has a lighter, fresher, and more vegetal flavor than traditional red rooibos.

Peppermint tea is an herbal infusion made from peppermint leaves. It has a strong cooling sensation and a fresh minty aroma. It is commonly consumed hot and is often served without milk.

Spearmint tea is an herbal infusion made from spearmint leaves. It generally has a milder and sweeter mint flavor than peppermint tea. It can be served hot or chilled.

Chamomile tea is an herbal infusion made from chamomile flowers. It has a mild floral, apple-like, and honey-like character. It is commonly consumed as a caffeine-free evening beverage.

Hibiscus tea is an infusion made from hibiscus calyces. It produces a vivid red or deep ruby-colored drink with a tart, fruity, cranberry-like flavor. It is commonly served hot or cold and can be sweetened.

Ginger tea is a beverage made by steeping fresh ginger, dried ginger, or ginger pieces in hot water. It has a warm, spicy, pungent flavor. Ginger tea can be combined with lemon, honey, mint, or other spices.

Lemongrass tea is an herbal infusion made from lemongrass stalks or leaves. It has a fresh citrus-like aroma and a light, grassy flavor. It is commonly served hot or cold.

Tulsi tea is an herbal infusion made from holy basil, also known as tulsi. It has an aromatic, herbal, peppery, and slightly clove-like character. It is commonly consumed in India either alone or blended with other herbs and tea leaves.

Rose tea is an infusion made from rose petals or rose buds. It generally has a delicate floral aroma and mild flavor. Rose can also be blended with black, green, or white tea.

Lavender tea is an herbal infusion made from lavender flowers. It has a strongly floral, aromatic, and slightly sweet flavor. Because the aroma can be intense, lavender is often blended with other herbs.

Lemon tea is tea flavored with lemon juice, lemon slices, or lemon peel. It can be prepared using black tea, green tea, or herbal infusions. Lemon adds acidity and citrus aroma and is commonly combined with sugar or honey.

Honey tea is tea sweetened with honey. It can refer to many different tea bases, including black tea, green tea, herbal tea, ginger tea, and lemon tea. The flavor depends on both the tea and the type of honey used.

Iced tea is tea that is cooled and served cold, often over ice. It can be made from black tea, green tea, oolong tea, herbal tea, or blended tea. It may be served plain or flavored with lemon, peach, mint, berries, or other ingredients.

Sweet tea is a style of iced tea that is heavily sweetened, particularly associated with the southern United States. The tea is generally brewed black tea that is sweetened while warm and then chilled.

Peach tea is tea flavored with peach juice, peach syrup, peach pieces, or peach flavoring. It is often prepared as an iced tea and has a sweet, fruity character.

Lemon iced tea is chilled tea flavored with lemon. It is commonly sweetened and served over ice. Black tea is frequently used as the base.

Milk tea is tea combined with milk. It can refer to many regional beverages, including Indian chai, British-style tea with milk, Hong Kong milk tea, and bubble tea. The tea-to-milk ratio and preparation method vary substantially.

Hong Kong milk tea is a strong milk tea traditionally associated with Hong Kong. It commonly uses a blend of black teas and evaporated or condensed milk. It is known for a strong tea flavor and creamy texture.

Thai tea is a sweet, creamy tea beverage associated with Thailand. A typical preparation uses strongly brewed tea combined with sweetened condensed milk and evaporated milk or another dairy ingredient. It is often served over ice and may contain spices or flavoring.

Bubble tea is a Taiwanese beverage category combining tea with milk or fruit flavors and chewy tapioca pearls or other toppings. It can be served hot or cold, but cold versions are especially common. Modern bubble tea shops offer many combinations of tea bases, sweetness levels, ice levels, toppings, and flavors.

Brown sugar milk tea is a milk tea flavored with brown sugar syrup. It often has a caramel-like sweetness and may be served with tapioca pearls. It is generally served cold, although warm versions exist.

Fruit tea is a tea-based beverage flavored with fruit, fruit juice, fruit syrup, or pieces of fruit. Common flavors include peach, lemon, mango, strawberry, passion fruit, and lychee. Fruit tea can be served hot or iced.

Herbal tea is a broad category of beverages made by infusing herbs, flowers, spices, fruits, roots, or other plant materials in water. Technically, many herbal teas are tisanes rather than true teas because they do not contain Camellia sinensis leaves.

Spiced tea is tea prepared with aromatic spices such as cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, ginger, star anise, nutmeg, or pepper. The term covers many regional preparations. Masala chai is one well-known example.

Butter tea is a traditional tea beverage associated particularly with Tibetan and Himalayan cultures. It is traditionally made using strong tea, butter, and salt. The drink is rich, savory, warming, and quite different from sweet milk tea.

Kashmiri kahwa is a traditional spiced tea associated with Kashmir. It commonly uses green tea, saffron, cardamom, cinnamon, nuts, and sometimes sugar or honey. It is aromatic and warming and is often served in small cups.

Kashmiri pink tea, also called noon chai, is a traditional tea associated with Kashmir. It is commonly prepared using green tea, salt, baking soda, milk, and sometimes additional ingredients. The preparation process gives it a distinctive pink color and a salty, creamy taste.

Moroccan mint tea is traditionally prepared with green tea, fresh mint, and sugar. It is commonly associated with Moroccan hospitality. The combination creates a sweet, refreshing, minty drink.

Russian tea is a broad term used for tea traditions involving strong brewed tea, sometimes accompanied by lemon, sugar, jam, or other additions. Historical Russian tea culture is strongly associated with the samovar.

Turkish tea is a strong black tea commonly prepared using a stacked double teapot called a çaydanlık. It is generally served in small tulip-shaped glasses and can be consumed plain or with sugar.

Persian tea is commonly a black tea preparation served in Iranian tea culture. It is usually brewed strong and may be served with sugar, rock candy, saffron, cardamom, or other flavorings.

South African rooibos tea is a caffeine-free herbal beverage made from rooibos. It can be consumed plain, with milk, with honey, or blended with spices and fruit flavors.

Drink varieties

Water is the most basic and widely consumed beverage. It may be consumed from natural sources after appropriate treatment or as commercially bottled or filtered water. Water contains no added calories when consumed plain and is essential for hydration.

Mineral water is water containing naturally occurring minerals and dissolved substances. The mineral composition varies according to the source. It may be naturally carbonated or still.

Sparkling water is water containing dissolved carbon dioxide, producing bubbles and a carbonated sensation. It can be naturally carbonated or artificially carbonated. It is commonly served plain or flavored.

Soda water is carbonated water, often with added mineral salts. It is frequently used as a mixer in non-alcoholic and alcoholic drinks.

Tonic water is a carbonated beverage traditionally containing quinine flavoring. Modern tonic water is generally sweetened and flavored and is commonly used as a mixer. It has a characteristic bitter taste.

Lemon water is plain water flavored with lemon juice or lemon slices. It can be served cold, at room temperature, or warm. It is a simple beverage with a citrus aroma and acidic taste.

Coconut water is the clear liquid found inside young green coconuts. It has a mild, slightly sweet flavor and contains naturally occurring electrolytes and minerals. It can be consumed chilled or used in smoothies and other beverages.

Milk is a nutrient-containing dairy beverage produced by mammals, with cow's milk being one of the most commonly consumed forms. Milk can be consumed plain or used as an ingredient in tea, coffee, smoothies, milkshakes, desserts, and other drinks.

Whole milk contains a relatively high proportion of milk fat compared with reduced-fat milk. It has a creamy texture and rich flavor.

Low-fat milk contains less milk fat than whole milk. It has a lighter texture while retaining many of the characteristics of dairy milk.

Skim milk has most of its milk fat removed. It has a relatively thin texture and lighter mouthfeel.

Buttermilk traditionally refers to the liquid left after churning butter from cream, while modern commercial cultured buttermilk is commonly produced by fermenting milk. It has a tangy flavor and thick texture.

Chaas is a traditional Indian yogurt-based drink commonly prepared by diluting yogurt with water and adding salt and spices. It is often flavored with roasted cumin, coriander, ginger, green chili, mint, or curry leaves. It is commonly served chilled.

Lassi is a yogurt-based beverage associated with South Asia. It can be sweet, salty, savory, or fruit-flavored. Common varieties include mango lassi, sweet lassi, salted lassi, rose lassi, and plain lassi.

Sweet lassi is a yogurt drink blended with water or milk and sweetener. It may contain cardamom, rose water, saffron, or fruit. It is usually served chilled.

Salted lassi is a savory yogurt drink containing salt and often spices or herbs. It is generally thinner and more savory than sweet lassi.

Mango lassi is a yogurt-based drink blended with mango pulp or mango pieces. It has a creamy texture and sweet tropical fruit flavor. It is commonly served chilled.

Rose lassi is lassi flavored with rose syrup or rose water. It has a sweet floral aroma and pink or reddish color when colored syrup is used.

Saffron lassi is lassi flavored with saffron. It may also contain cardamom, sugar, or nuts. The drink is creamy, aromatic, and mildly floral.

Coffee is a beverage prepared from roasted coffee beans, which are the seeds of coffee cherries. Different preparation methods produce different flavors, strengths, textures, and aromas. Common coffee styles include espresso, Americano, cappuccino, latte, mocha, and cold brew.

Espresso is a concentrated coffee beverage produced by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure. It has a concentrated flavor and is commonly served in small quantities. Espresso also forms the base of many milk and coffee beverages.

Americano is generally made by combining espresso with hot water. It has a lighter concentration than straight espresso while retaining espresso-derived flavors.

Long black is a coffee beverage generally prepared by adding espresso to hot water. The preparation order differs from an Americano in many traditions, which can affect the crema and presentation.

Cappuccino is an espresso-based milk beverage traditionally made with espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam. It has a strong coffee character combined with creamy texture and foam.

Latte is an espresso-based beverage containing a relatively large amount of steamed milk and a smaller layer of foam. It is generally milder and creamier than a cappuccino.

Flat white is an espresso and steamed milk beverage characterized by a relatively thin layer of fine-textured microfoam. It typically has a stronger coffee-to-milk impression than a large latte.

Macchiato is an espresso beverage with a small amount of milk or milk foam. The term can describe different preparations, including traditional espresso macchiato and larger caramel-style beverages in some commercial contexts.

Mocha is a coffee beverage combining espresso, milk, and chocolate. It may be topped with milk foam, whipped cream, chocolate powder, or chocolate syrup. It has a combination of coffee, cocoa, sweetness, and creamy dairy flavor.

Cortado is a small coffee beverage combining espresso with approximately an equal or similar amount of warm milk. The milk reduces espresso acidity and intensity while preserving a strong coffee character.

Affogato is a dessert-like beverage made by pouring hot espresso over cold ice cream or gelato. The contrast between hot coffee and cold creamy dessert creates a distinctive texture and flavor.

Cold brew coffee is coffee brewed using cool or room-temperature water over a relatively long extraction period. It usually has a smooth, less sharp flavor compared with some hot-brewed coffees. It is commonly served over ice and may be diluted or combined with milk.

Iced coffee is coffee served chilled, usually over ice. It can be prepared by brewing coffee hot and cooling it or by using other methods. It may be served black, with milk, sugar, cream, or flavored syrups.

Frappé is a chilled blended or shaken coffee beverage. Different regional versions exist. It can contain instant coffee, milk, sugar, ice, or other ingredients and is commonly served cold.

Vietnamese iced coffee is commonly prepared using strong coffee brewed through a Vietnamese drip filter and combined with sweetened condensed milk. It is typically served over ice and has a strong, sweet, creamy flavor.

Irish coffee is a coffee beverage traditionally combining hot coffee, whiskey, sugar, and cream. It is served as a layered or topped drink in many preparations.

Turkish coffee is a method of preparing finely ground coffee by simmering or heating it with water, traditionally in a small pot called a cezve or ibrik. The grounds remain in the cup, creating a strong, concentrated beverage with a distinctive texture.

Greek coffee is closely related to the Turkish-style method and is prepared using finely ground coffee heated with water, often with sugar. It is traditionally served in small cups with the grounds settling at the bottom.

Hot chocolate is a warm beverage made from cocoa or chocolate combined with milk or water. It can be thick and rich or relatively light. Common additions include sugar, whipped cream, marshmallows, cinnamon, vanilla, or chocolate shavings.

Cocoa is a beverage made primarily from cocoa powder combined with milk or water and usually sweetener. Compared with melted-chocolate drinks, cocoa powder-based beverages can have a lighter body.

Chocolate milk is milk flavored with cocoa and sweetener. It is commonly served chilled but can also be served warm.

Milkshake is a thick cold beverage generally made by blending milk with ice cream and flavorings. Common flavors include vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, mango, banana, and cookies. Milkshakes may be topped with whipped cream or other ingredients.

Vanilla milkshake is a milkshake flavored primarily with vanilla. It is creamy, sweet, and aromatic and is commonly made using vanilla ice cream and milk.

Chocolate milkshake is a milkshake containing chocolate ice cream, cocoa, chocolate syrup, or melted chocolate. It has a rich chocolate flavor and creamy texture.

Strawberry milkshake is a milkshake flavored with strawberries or strawberry syrup. It combines sweet dairy flavor with fruity strawberry notes.

Mango shake is a thick beverage made by blending mango pulp or mango pieces with milk. It is particularly popular in regions where mangoes are widely consumed. Sugar or ice cream may be added for additional sweetness and richness.

Banana shake is made by blending banana with milk. It has a thick texture and naturally sweet flavor. It may be flavored with cinnamon, honey, dates, or nuts.

Fruit juice is liquid extracted from fruits. It can be consumed fresh or commercially processed. Common varieties include orange juice, apple juice, grape juice, pineapple juice, mango juice, and pomegranate juice.

Orange juice is juice made from oranges. It has a sweet and acidic citrus flavor and is commonly consumed at breakfast. It may be fresh, chilled, pulpy, or filtered.

Apple juice is juice made from apples. It has a sweet, fruity flavor and can range from clear to cloudy depending on processing.

Mango juice is a sweet tropical fruit beverage made from mango pulp or mango juice. It is commonly thick, fragrant, and rich in fruit flavor.

Pineapple juice is juice made from pineapple. It has a sweet and acidic tropical flavor and can be consumed alone or mixed into other beverages.

Grape juice is juice made from grapes. It can be red, purple, green, or white depending on the grape variety. It is generally sweet and fruity.

Pomegranate juice is juice extracted from pomegranate arils. It has a deep red color and a flavor that combines sweetness and acidity with mild astringency.

Guava juice is made from guava pulp or guava fruit. It can be thick and fragrant, with a sweet tropical flavor. It is commonly consumed chilled.

Watermelon juice is made by blending or pressing watermelon. It has a high water content and a naturally sweet, refreshing flavor. It is often served cold.

Papaya juice is made from ripe papaya pulp blended with water or other fruit ingredients. It has a soft tropical flavor and thick texture.

Lychee juice is a sweet fruit beverage made from lychee fruit or lychee juice. It has a fragrant floral and tropical character.

Cranberry juice is made from cranberries or cranberry concentrate. It has a strongly tart flavor and is frequently sweetened or blended with other juices.

Tomato juice is a savory vegetable-based beverage made from tomatoes. It may be seasoned with salt, pepper, celery, lemon, or other ingredients. It is sometimes used in savory mixed drinks.

Carrot juice is made from carrots and has a naturally sweet, earthy flavor. It can be consumed alone or combined with orange, apple, ginger, lemon, or other ingredients.

Beetroot juice is made from beetroot and has a deep red color and earthy, sweet flavor. It is commonly combined with apple, carrot, lemon, or ginger.

Celery juice is made from celery stalks and has a fresh, vegetal, salty, and slightly bitter flavor. It is often consumed chilled.

Lemonade is a drink made primarily from lemon juice, water, and a sweetener. It has a refreshing combination of acidity and sweetness. It can be served chilled and may be flavored with mint, berries, ginger, or other ingredients.

Pink lemonade is lemonade with a pink or reddish color. The color may come from fruit juices, berries, botanical ingredients, or food coloring. It generally retains the sweet and tart character of ordinary lemonade.

Mint lemonade is lemonade flavored with fresh mint. The citrus acidity combines with the cool, herbal aroma of mint. It is commonly served over ice.

Ginger lemonade is lemonade flavored with fresh ginger or ginger syrup. It combines lemon acidity with warm ginger spice.

Limeade is a beverage similar to lemonade but made primarily with lime juice. It has a sharper citrus flavor and is commonly sweetened and served chilled.

Aam panna is a traditional Indian summer beverage made from raw green mangoes, water, sugar or another sweetener, and spices. Common seasonings include roasted cumin, black salt, and mint. It has a tart, sweet, salty, and spicy flavor.

Jaljeera is a spiced Indian beverage commonly made using water and a mixture of cumin, mint, black salt, tamarind, ginger, and other spices. It has a tangy, savory, spicy, and refreshing flavor.

Sugarcane juice is extracted from fresh sugarcane stalks. It is naturally sweet and often served chilled with lemon, ginger, or other flavorings. It is commonly sold as a fresh street beverage in parts of South Asia.

Nimbu pani is a South Asian lemon drink made from lemon juice, water, and sweetener or salt. It may be sweet, salty, or a combination of both. It is commonly served chilled.

Sharbat is a broad category of sweetened fruit, flower, herb, or syrup-based beverages common in South Asia and the Middle East. It is generally diluted with water before serving. Flavors include rose, khus, lemon, sandalwood, and fruit.

Rooh Afza-style rose drink is a sweet rose-flavored beverage prepared by mixing rose-based syrup with water or milk. It has a strong floral aroma and bright color.

Falooda is a layered South Asian dessert beverage. It commonly contains milk, sweet syrup, basil seeds, vermicelli noodles, ice cream or kulfi, and nuts. Different regional versions use different ingredients.

Badam milk is a milk-based beverage flavored with almonds. It may contain saffron, cardamom, sugar, or other nuts. It can be served warm or chilled.

Pista milk is milk flavored with pistachios, often combined with cardamom and saffron. It has a creamy, nutty flavor and can be served cold or warm.

Thandai is a traditional Indian milk-based beverage associated with festivals and celebrations. It commonly contains milk, nuts, seeds, spices, saffron, and sweetener. The spice mixture can include cardamom, fennel, pepper, and rose petals.

Kesar milk is milk flavored with saffron, often sweetened and sometimes combined with cardamom or nuts. It can be served warm or chilled.

Smoothie is a blended beverage made from fruits, vegetables, milk, yogurt, plant-based milk, juice, ice, or other ingredients. Smoothies generally have a thicker texture than juice because much of the fruit or vegetable material remains in the drink.

Fruit smoothie is a smoothie primarily made from fruits. Common combinations include banana and strawberry, mango and pineapple, mixed berries, and peach and yogurt.

Green smoothie is a smoothie containing leafy greens or other green vegetables along with fruit and liquid ingredients. Spinach, kale, cucumber, apple, banana, and lemon are common components.

Berry smoothie is a smoothie made with berries such as strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, or blackberries. It generally has a sweet-tart fruit flavor and colorful appearance.

Mango smoothie is a thick blended drink made with mango and ingredients such as yogurt, milk, coconut milk, or juice. It has a creamy tropical flavor.

Protein shake is a blended beverage containing a concentrated source of protein, such as milk, yogurt, protein powder, or other protein-rich ingredients. It may also contain fruit, oats, nut butter, or other ingredients.

Mocktail is a non-alcoholic mixed beverage designed to resemble a cocktail in presentation or complexity. It may contain fruit juices, syrups, herbs, spices, carbonated water, tea, or other ingredients.

Virgin mojito is a non-alcoholic drink inspired by the mojito. It commonly combines lime juice, fresh mint, sugar or syrup, and soda water. It is usually served over ice.

Virgin piña colada is a non-alcoholic tropical drink made with pineapple juice and coconut cream or coconut milk. It is usually blended or shaken with ice.

Virgin Mary is a non-alcoholic version of the Bloody Mary. It commonly contains tomato juice, lemon juice, spices, hot sauce, and savory seasonings.

Fruit punch is a mixed fruit beverage combining several juices or fruit-flavored ingredients. It may include orange, pineapple, mango, apple, cranberry, or other fruit flavors.

Iced fruit punch is fruit punch served chilled, often over ice. It is commonly used as a party beverage and may be garnished with fresh fruit.

Energy drink is a beverage formulated to provide stimulating or energizing effects, commonly through caffeine and other ingredients. Many energy drinks also contain sugar or artificial sweeteners, flavorings, acids, and vitamins.

Sports drink is a beverage designed to provide fluids and electrolytes, often with carbohydrates. It is commonly consumed during or after prolonged physical activity.

Electrolyte drink is a beverage containing minerals such as sodium and potassium. It may be formulated for hydration during situations involving substantial fluid or electrolyte loss.

Malt drink is a beverage made using malted grains or malt extracts. It can be non-alcoholic or alcoholic depending on the product and fermentation process. Non-alcoholic malt beverages are often sweet and carbonated.

Ginger ale is a carbonated soft drink flavored with ginger. It is generally sweet and lightly spicy. It can be consumed alone or used as a mixer.

Ginger beer is a ginger-flavored beverage traditionally produced through fermentation, although many modern commercial versions are non-alcoholic and carbonated without significant fermentation. It generally has a stronger ginger flavor than ginger ale.

Root beer is a sweet carbonated soft drink traditionally flavored with roots, bark, herbs, and spices. Modern formulations vary widely and are commonly caffeine-free, although some products may contain caffeine.

Cola is a carbonated soft drink typically flavored with caramel color, citrus oils, spices, and other flavorings. Many cola beverages contain caffeine, although caffeine-free versions are also available.

Orange soda is a carbonated soft drink flavored primarily with orange. It generally has a sweet, fruity, citrus flavor and bright orange color.

Lemon-lime soda is a carbonated soft drink flavored with lemon and lime. It is usually clear or lightly colored and has a sweet, acidic, citrus flavor.

Hot beverage is a drink normally served warm or hot. Common examples include tea, coffee, hot chocolate, mulled beverages, herbal infusions, and warm milk drinks.

Cold beverage is a drink normally served chilled or over ice. Examples include iced tea, iced coffee, lemonade, fruit juice, smoothies, soft drinks, and milkshakes.

Fermented beverage is a drink produced through microbial fermentation. Fermentation may change sugars into acids, gases, or alcohol depending on the microorganisms and process. Examples include kombucha, kefir, kvass, and certain alcoholic beverages.

Alcoholic beverage is a drink containing ethanol produced through fermentation, distillation, or both. Major categories include beer, wine, cider, spirits, and mixed alcoholic drinks.

Beer is an alcoholic beverage generally produced by fermenting malted grains with yeast and flavoring the beverage with hops or other ingredients. Beer styles differ widely in color, bitterness, aroma, alcohol content, and fermentation method.

Lager is a beer style generally fermented and conditioned using lager yeast at relatively cool temperatures. Common lager styles include pilsner, helles, dunkel, and märzen.

Ale is a broad category of beer traditionally produced using ale yeast at warmer fermentation temperatures. Ales can include pale ale, IPA, stout, porter, and many other styles.

Pilsner is a pale lager style originating in Central Europe. It is commonly characterized by a crisp body, noticeable hop aroma or bitterness, and a clean finish.

Stout is a dark ale style often characterized by roasted malt flavors. It may have notes of coffee, cocoa, toast, caramel, or dark grain.

Porter is a dark beer style with roasted and malt-driven flavors. It can have chocolate, coffee, caramel, nutty, or toasted characteristics.

IPA, or India Pale Ale, is a hop-forward beer style. It often has pronounced hop bitterness and aromas of citrus, tropical fruit, pine, flowers, or herbs, depending on the hop varieties and brewing method.

Wheat beer is beer made with a significant proportion of wheat in the grain bill. It can have a soft body and may show bread-like, fruity, spicy, or creamy characteristics.

Cider is an alcoholic beverage made by fermenting apple juice. Cider styles range from sweet to dry and from still to highly carbonated. Some ciders contain other fruits or spices.

Perry is an alcoholic beverage made by fermenting pear juice. It can range from sweet and fruity to dry and complex.

Wine is an alcoholic beverage made primarily by fermenting grapes or grape juice. Wine can be red, white, rosé, sparkling, or fortified, with many regional styles.

Red wine is wine made primarily from dark-colored grape varieties and typically fermented with grape skins. It commonly has flavors and aromas associated with fruit, spices, herbs, oak, earth, or tannins.

White wine is wine usually made from green or yellow grapes, although some dark grapes can also be used when the skins are removed. It commonly ranges from crisp and acidic to rich and creamy.

Rosé wine is wine with a pink color produced through limited contact between grape juice and dark grape skins. It generally has fresh fruit and floral characteristics.

Sparkling wine is wine containing significant dissolved carbon dioxide, creating bubbles. Champagne is one specific type of sparkling wine, while many other regions produce their own sparkling styles.

Fortified wine is wine to which distilled spirit has been added. Examples include port, sherry, Madeira, and certain styles of vermouth.

Whiskey is a distilled alcoholic beverage made from fermented grain mash and generally aged in wooden barrels. Major traditions include Scotch whisky, Irish whiskey, bourbon, rye whiskey, and Japanese whisky.

Vodka is a distilled spirit traditionally produced from grains, potatoes, or other agricultural materials. It is generally relatively neutral in aroma and flavor and is widely used in mixed drinks.

Rum is a distilled spirit made from sugarcane products such as molasses or sugarcane juice. Styles range from light and relatively neutral to dark, aged, rich, and aromatic.

Gin is a distilled spirit characterized primarily by juniper flavor. Different gins may contain botanicals such as coriander, citrus peel, angelica, cardamom, and herbs.

Brandy is a distilled spirit made from fermented fruit juice or wine. Grape brandy is common, but brandy can also be made from apples, pears, cherries, and other fruits.

Tequila is a distilled spirit produced from blue Weber agave and associated with Mexico. It can be classified into styles including blanco, reposado, añejo, and extra añejo based on production and aging.

Mezcal is a Mexican distilled spirit made from agave. Different agave varieties and production methods can create a wide range of flavors, often including earthy, smoky, fruity, vegetal, or mineral notes.

Cocktail is a mixed alcoholic beverage combining spirits, liqueurs, wine, bitters, juices, syrups, soda, herbs, spices, or other ingredients. Cocktails can be shaken, stirred, blended, built directly in a glass, or prepared using other techniques.

Margarita is a cocktail commonly made with tequila, orange liqueur, and lime juice. It is often served over ice or blended and may be served with a salted rim.

Martini is a classic cocktail generally associated with gin or vodka and vermouth. It may be served with an olive or lemon twist, depending on the preparation.

Old Fashioned is a whiskey cocktail traditionally made with whiskey, sugar, and bitters. It is commonly served over ice and garnished with citrus peel.

Negroni is a cocktail traditionally made with gin, sweet vermouth, and Campari in roughly equal proportions. It has a bitter, herbal, citrusy, and spirit-forward flavor.

Daiquiri is a classic cocktail based on rum, lime juice, and sugar. It is traditionally shaken and served chilled.

Manhattan is a cocktail generally made with whiskey, sweet vermouth, and bitters. It is commonly stirred and served chilled, often with a cherry garnish.

Bloody Mary is a savory cocktail commonly made with vodka and tomato juice, along with ingredients such as lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, salt, pepper, and celery. Recipes vary widely.

Piña colada is a tropical cocktail traditionally made with rum, pineapple juice, and coconut cream or coconut milk. It is generally blended or shaken with ice.

Moscow Mule is a cocktail commonly made with vodka, ginger beer, and lime juice. It is typically served over ice and is known for its spicy ginger and citrus flavor.

Punch is a broad category of mixed beverages made from combinations of spirits, wine, tea, juices, fruit, spices, sugar, and water or other mixers. Both alcoholic and non-alcoholic punches exist.
